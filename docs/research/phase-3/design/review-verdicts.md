SUMMARY: The bench design is written and prototyped; four things change the spec or need the owner before planning.

**Spec changes that matter most**
- **Stream needs a no-timing route (7.2).** An open stream is stopped by a timed key press, which WCAG 2.1.1 does not allow as the only route. I added "Run in 1 mL" and "Run in 5 mL", which close by themselves. Stream is a toggle, and a held key adds one drop.
- **"Rough position" must be five positions (11).** Fewer cannot keep a text-only learner within ±0.02 mL. With five, the worst error over every level from 0.000 to 50.000 mL is exactly 0.020 mL.
- **Drawn pink cannot be proportional (7.3).** The threshold would be drawn at about 3%. I draw nothing below the threshold, then 1 − exp(−c / 1e-5 M).
- **Save every stream addition (10).** Otherwise a reload undoes a stream.

**Owner decisions**
- Parallax: I left it out.
- Guided closes a running stream when the "slow down" hint first shows: recommended.
- The meniscus in words is open to every learner: recommended.
- Stream speed, and how pink the endpoint looks: my values need the owner's eye.
